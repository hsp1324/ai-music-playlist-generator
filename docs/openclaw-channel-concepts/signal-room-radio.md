# OpenClaw Channel Concept Planner: Signal Room Radio

Use this after the selected channel is `Signal Room Radio`, or while the connected channel still appears as legacy `AI썰전`. This document decides the next playlist concept. Use `../openclaw-channel-profiles/signal-room-radio.md` afterward for cover, thumbnail, and 10 second loop-video production rules.

## Channel Promise

Signal Room Radio is a no-vocal analytical/story BGM channel. It makes instrumental music for research, script writing, debate prep, fact-checking, data review, mystery documentaries, story videos, note-taking, planning, and deep analytical work.

The proven reference release is:

```text
[playlist] AI 토론 준비 BGM | 리서치·대본 정리를 위한 집중 음악
```

Keep making music with this sound DNA, but do not trap every release inside one narrow keyword. The sound should feel like a future Korean AI debate studio, a midnight research room, a calm newsroom, a mystery documentary desk, or a briefing room where someone is preparing a clear argument. It should be focused, warm, modern, lightly tense, and useful for thinking. Mystery BGM and documentary BGM are valid as long as the music stays clean, smart, instrumental, and not horror/trailer music.

## Channel Name

Recommended public channel name:

```text
Signal Room Radio
```

Why this name fits:

- `Signal` suggests data, broadcast, analysis, news, and useful information.
- `Room` is broader than `Desk`: it can be a research room, signal room, studio, briefing room, archive room, or mystery-documentary room.
- `Radio` keeps it aligned with the existing music-channel family.
- It is broader and more reusable than `AI썰전`, but still supports AI/debate/research concepts.
- It is broader than `Signal Desk Radio` and less locked to one desk/work keyword, while still leaving room for mystery/documentary BGM.

If `/youtube/status` still shows `AI썰전`, treat it as this same connected channel. Use `Signal Room Radio` in cover labels, thumbnail branding, metadata planning, and Slack reports until the human manually renames/reconnects the YouTube channel. `Signal Desk Radio` and `Midnight Cue Radio` are legacy draft names only.

## Reference Sound DNA

The existing `AI썰전` release worked because it repeatedly used:

- Warm analog synth pulse.
- Felt piano motifs.
- Rhodes/electric piano chords.
- Muted electronic percussion.
- Soft breakbeat, soft trip-hop, restrained garage beat, tiny brush kit, or light shaker.
- Glassy arpeggios, soft plucked synth, clocklike mallets, marimba plucks, or muted guitar harmonics.
- Round bass or smooth sub bass, never aggressive.
- Low analytical tension: focused and intelligent, but not stressful.
- Around 84-104 BPM, usually 88-100 BPM.
- Suno duration wording should be minimal: use only `less than 4 minutes` or `under 4 minutes` when a duration hint is needed. Do not add exact ranges, lower-bound targets, or any extra ending/completion wording to prompts, style strings, lyrics, or bracketed metatags. The helper rejects playlist tracks over 4 minutes by default.

Use these elements as the default style vocabulary. Vary the instruments slightly from track to track, but do not leave this family.

## Required Suno Direction

Use no-vocal instrumental format. Every track must include an explicit no-vocal line:

```text
[Instrumental only: no sung words, no humming, no spoken words]
```

Use bracket-only structure blocks in the lyrics/custom-lyrics field:

```text
[Intro: ...]
[Main Theme: ...]
[Development: ...]
[Instrumental Break: ...]
[Bridge: ...]
[Final Theme: ...]
[Outro: closing section]
[End]
```

Suno style prompts should be close to this template:

```text
instrumental only analytical story BGM, no vocals no humming no spoken words, less than 4 minutes, warm analog synth pulse, felt piano motifs, Rhodes chords, muted electronic percussion, glassy arpeggios, round bass, low analytical tension
```

Good style variations:

- `instrumental only tech research BGM, less than 4 minutes, soft marimba plucks, Rhodes chords, light shaker, muted kick, precise but calm`
- `instrumental only quiet debate prep BGM, less than 4 minutes, brushed jazz-electronic drums, Rhodes, muted trumpet-like synth lead, walking soft bass`
- `instrumental only search-and-archive BGM, less than 4 minutes, soft breakbeat, Rhodes pad, muted guitar harmonics, airy synth`
- `instrumental only calm debate-clock music, less than 4 minutes, soft ticking percussion, plucked synth, felt piano, round bass`
- `instrumental only midnight analysis BGM, less than 4 minutes, soft trip-hop drums, felt piano, muted bass, blue synth pad`
- `instrumental only mystery documentary BGM, less than 4 minutes, Rhodes pad, soft mallets, restrained pulse, warm analog synth, low analytical tension`
- `instrumental only data-rain focus music, less than 4 minutes, rainy synth plucks, soft electric piano, restrained garage beat, sub bass`

## Concept Lanes

Choose one lane per release. Stay close to the reference sound, but rotate the story/use case:

- AI debate prep: research desk, rebuttal notes, calm argument building.
- Script room: outline writing, cue sheets, narration prep, source checks.
- Fact-checking: reference stacks, citation grids, archive windows.
- Data review: dashboards, charts, signal rooms, data rain, quiet analytics.
- Briefing room: late-night panel prep, blue desk logic, final draft resolve.
- Newsroom focus: future newsroom, upload window light, editorial desk.
- Whiteboard method: markers, clocks, structured thinking, deep focus.
- Mystery documentary: archive room, quiet clues, source review, restrained investigative tension.
- Story analysis: timeline review, narration prep, documentary planning, quiet evidence sorting.

Avoid gore, real victim stories, exploitative true-crime framing, jump-scare sound design, fantasy adventure, cafe/sleep/chill-only BGM, or pop vocals unless the human explicitly changes the channel direction. Mystery/noir/documentary words are allowed when the music remains clean, intelligent, and useful as background BGM.

## Freshness Rules

From `scripts/openclaw-release list-releases`, inspect recent `Signal Room Radio`, `Signal Desk Radio`, `AI썰전`, or legacy `Midnight Cue Radio` releases and avoid repeating:

- The same situation, such as debate prep, script writing, fact-checking, data review, briefing room, mystery archive, or documentary planning.
- The same thumbnail phrase, such as `AI DEBATE`, `RESEARCH`, `SCRIPT NOTES`, `FACT CHECK`, `DEEP WORK`, `DATA REVIEW`, `NEWSROOM`, `MYSTERY`, `DOCUMENTARY`, or `SIGNAL ROOM`.
- The same main instrument combination for the whole release.
- The same visual scene, such as one blue desk, one hologram table, one archive shelf, or one newsroom monitor wall.

The sound may stay very consistent, but the concept angle and visual scene should rotate.

## Good Title Shapes

Use `[playlist]` and a clear use case. Do not make every title only about research, debate, or script notes. Titles should be broad enough for viewers who want mystery/documentary/focus BGM, while still matching the actual music:

- `[playlist] AI Debate Prep BGM | Research, Script Notes and Deep Focus Music`
- `[playlist] Future Newsroom Focus BGM | Fact-Checking, Writing and Data Review`
- `[playlist] Midnight Research Desk BGM | Script Planning, Source Checks and Quiet Work`
- `[playlist] Signal Room Focus BGM | Data Review, Whiteboard Notes and Analysis Music`
- `[playlist] Briefing Room BGM | Calm Tech-Newsroom Music for Deep Work`
- `[playlist] Mystery Documentary BGM | Archive Room, Quiet Clues and Deep Focus`
- `[playlist] Story Analysis BGM | Timeline Notes, Source Checks and Calm Tension`
- `[playlist] Signal Room Mystery BGM | Research, Documentary Notes and Night Focus`

Korean titles can be used when the default language is Korean, but the channel identity should still be `Signal Room Radio`.

## Bad Directions

- Locking every release to one narrow keyword such as AI debate, script writing, or mystery only.
- Exploitative true-crime positioning with real victims/suspects.
- Narrated debate videos or AI commentary until a separate narration/video pipeline exists.
- Pop vocals, sung hooks, humming, spoken words, whispering, or producer tags.
- Sleep-only, cafe-only, or nature-only BGM that belongs on Soft Hour Radio.
- Generic titles like `AI Music`, `Dark BGM`, `Focus Playlist`, or `Tech BGM` by themselves.
