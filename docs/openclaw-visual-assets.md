# OpenClaw Visual Asset Skills

Use this when OpenClaw creates cover images, YouTube thumbnails, short loop videos, or still-image render packages for this repo.

The production source of truth is [openclaw-channel-profiles](openclaw-channel-profiles/README.md). Run `scripts/openclaw-release channel-profile` first, then read the returned `profile_doc`.

## Shared Asset Contract

- Create static cover and thumbnail images with OpenAI GPT Image models, not Dreamina.
- Use Gemini only for moving loop videos. Do not open or use Dreamina, Seedance, or CapCut video generation for new automation work. If Gemini is unavailable, on cooldown, blocked after safe retries, or cannot create a usable MP4 within the wait window, use the approved `still_image` fallback instead of deferring or switching providers. HaruHaru, sundaze, Solwave Radio, Tokyo Daydream Radio photorealistic Japanese hip-hop/R&B/rap releases, Club Bloom, and 불송 skip provider loop videos during normal automation and use `still_image` render.
- Always create the final 16:9 cover/first-frame first, then create the YouTube thumbnail from that image as a reference/edit derivative.
- Keep cover, thumbnail, and loop video or still-image render visuals visually continuous: same subject count, subject placement, silhouettes, clothing colors, props, background landmarks, lighting, palette, and camera angle.
- Do not put the YouTube channel name, channel logo, or channel-brand line on covers, thumbnails, first-frame images, or loop videos.
- If text is useful, use only a short natural style, genre, use-case, or scripture-passage phrase allowed by the selected channel profile, such as `J-POP`, `TECH HOUSE`, `CINEMATIC ORCHESTRA`, `GAME OST`, `Genesis 1:1-5`, or `Matthew 1:18-25`. 불송 remains text-free by default and must not use Buddhist source or technical terms in visible image text. Typography must sit directly on the artwork with no filled box, plaque, badge, pill, sticker, or detached background shape.
- Provider loop videos must preserve that transparent-text treatment. If the first frame already contains a short phrase, keep the letters directly on the scene; do not add, thicken, animate in, or preserve any black box, translucent dark panel, gradient scrim, rectangle, sticker, badge, pill, capsule, or filled label shape behind the text. Reject/regenerate provider clips that wrap existing text in a new panel even when the still image was clean.
- Do not add title sentences, lyrics, subtitles, duration text, UI, logos, watermarks, or unrelated words to the cover/first-frame or loop video.
- If Gemini/Veo adds its own provider logo or watermark, usually in the bottom-right corner, accept it as an unavoidable provider artifact. Do not regenerate only because that provider logo is present.
- Do not include spectrum bars, waveform graphics, equalizers, or audio meters in generated assets. The app adds the audio-reactive visualizer during final render.
- Every channel except Club Bloom uses the HaruHaru `bars` spectrum during final app render. Club Bloom uses the clean full-width bottom `club-floor-cyan` visualizer for modern electronic releases and may rotate through the other `club-floor-*` presets. A matching retro/vinyl release may occasionally use `club-crate-notes`; do not use it consecutively or on other channels.
- Human visual requests override the channel default when they are safe and compatible with the channel.
- All generated visuals should look animated, anime, illustrated, or stylized unless a channel profile says otherwise. `Soft Hour Radio` alternates two equal lanes: high-resolution photorealistic real-world BGM environments for `classic_piano`, and bright original Japanese-animation/game-key-art illustration for `anime_game_ost`; both use locked-off camera video with no camera movement. `HaruHaru` uses theme-first creative still images and may select photorealistic, cinematic, animated/illustrated, graphic, object-led, spatial, or surreal art per release. `sundaze` uses photorealistic English/American friend-taken smartphone lifestyle still images by default. `Solwave Radio` uses photorealistic Latin/Spanish friend-taken smartphone lifestyle still images by default. `Club Bloom` uses photorealistic premium nightlife still images by default. `Cinematic Pulse` uses original photorealistic cinematic or premium game-animation first-frame art and a Gemini loop whose camera/subject motion joins naturally at the repeat boundary. `불송` uses theme-first text-free app-rendered still images in the strongest mainstream Korean-song visual medium for each release; its Buddhist source/theme remains internal and public artwork avoids religious/devotional cues unless the human explicitly requests them.

## Loop Video Rules

- Before clicking Generate in Gemini, record the provider attempt with `scripts/openclaw-release provider-video-start --release-id RELEASE_ID --first-frame ABSOLUTE_FIRST_FRAME_IMAGE_PATH --provider gemini`. This command hashes the first-frame image and blocks duplicate same-release/same-image Gemini starts while another attempt is still inside its wait window.
- After starting a Gemini generation, wait for Gemini to finish and keep the OpenClaw lock alive with heartbeats while waiting.
- Check waiting state with `scripts/openclaw-release provider-video-status --release-id RELEASE_ID --first-frame ABSOLUTE_FIRST_FRAME_IMAGE_PATH`. If `wait_remaining_seconds` is greater than 0, keep waiting and do not start any other video provider. After 20 minutes with no usable Gemini MP4, mark Gemini timed out and use the approved still-image fallback for that release.
- When Gemini succeeds, run `scripts/openclaw-release provider-video-finish --release-id RELEASE_ID --first-frame ABSOLUTE_FIRST_FRAME_IMAGE_PATH --provider gemini --status succeeded --output-video ABSOLUTE_GEMINI_MP4` and upload that MP4. When Gemini rejects the prompt immediately, mark it `failed` and retry Gemini with a safer prompt when quota remains. When Gemini is still generating after 20 minutes, mark it `timed_out` and switch to still-image render. Never start Dreamina, Seedance, or CapCut as a fallback.
- HaruHaru normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload the approved theme-led still cover/thumbnail package, then render with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 1080p --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style editorial-lower-left`.
- sundaze normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload photorealistic English/American friend-taken phone-photo still cover/thumbnail images, then render with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 1080p --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style editorial-lower-left`.
- Solwave Radio normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload photorealistic friend-taken phone-photo still cover/thumbnail images, then render with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 1080p --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style editorial-lower-left`. Solwave MP4 visuals use the still image plus lower-left lyrics and lower-right spectrum by default.
- Club Bloom normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload a photorealistic still cover/thumbnail that fits the music: modern nightlife for electronic lanes, or a modern editorial record bar/vinyl room/listening booth/practice room for retro DJ lanes. Render at 1080p with a suitable `club-floor-*` preset, or occasionally `club-crate-notes` for subdued boom-bap/jazz-hop/trip-hop/rare-groove/vinyl moods.
- Gemini clips are uploaded as generated after inspection. Do not mention duration in Gemini prompts. Tag them with `--loop-video-provider gemini`; the app will render Gemini-tagged clips with a 2.0 second crossfade.
- Do not put `10 seconds`, `5 seconds`, `16:9`, `720p`, `1080p`, `loop`, `seamless loop`, `repeat`, or `cyclic` in the generation prompt. Set those in provider controls when available.
- Do not use a local pan/zoom, app still-image animation, or other workaround when video generation fails unless the human explicitly approves a still-image fallback.

## Prompt Stability Rules

- Describe the visible state, camera, composition, and environmental motion. Avoid conceptual phrases that imply a staged montage.
- Avoid `playlist`, `music visual`, `visualizer shot`, and scripture-framework words in the video prompt when they are not visually necessary. For 불송, prompt the mainstream emotion and visible scene rather than its internal source, such as `an unopened letter casting two incompatible shadows across a nearly empty night bus seat, cinematic blue-hour realism, quiet tension, one clear focal idea`. This is an example of a song-linked visual metaphor, not a reusable template.
- A fixed shot is the safest default for channels that require one, but the selected channel profile may allow moving-camera and subject continuity. Describe how the closing motion should hand off naturally to the opening motion instead of assuming every loop needs a locked camera or fixed subject framing.
- Keep negative lists short. Too many `do not` clauses can make providers focus on the forbidden action and cause hard cuts, resets, or layout changes.
- Ask for ambient motion that naturally exists in the first frame: light flicker, rain ripple, incense smoke, water shimmer, fabric/hair wind, city lights, DJ LEDs, lantern glow, leaves, clouds, or reflections.
- Reject/regenerate clips that change to a different scene, reset composition mid-clip, introduce extra text, invent channel names, or make the subject noticeably zoom in/out when the profile requires stable scale.
- Reject/regenerate clips that add a black or semi-transparent background behind first-frame text, turn plain integrated typography into a badge/label, or make the text flicker between transparent and boxed states.
- For `Cinematic Pulse`, boundary continuity overrides any fixed-camera or fixed-subject preference. The camera may be locked or may pan, tilt, track, dolly, truck, orbit, crane, zoom, or follow. The subject may change screen position, size, distance, pose, and facing direction.
- For `Cinematic Pulse`, transient subjects may enter and leave during the shot. A bird flying into frame and exiting before the end is valid when the beginning and ending base scene remain compatible.
- For `Cinematic Pulse`, the final frame need not match the first pixel-for-pixel. Keep the closing base composition, background, camera viewpoint, lighting, and color state broadly similar to the opening base scene and avoid a large unrelated scene change. Changes during the clip are valid; reject only an obvious discontinuity at the join.
- Never put `loop`, `loop video`, `seamless loop`, `repeat`, or `cyclic` in a Cinematic Pulse Gemini prompt. Describe one uninterrupted shot and the broadly similar opening/closing base scene instead. The app repeats and crossfades the downloaded Gemini clip.
- Preview every Cinematic Pulse Gemini MP4 at least three times back-to-back before upload. Reject/regenerate it if either join shows a camera/subject teleport, incompatible direction or speed, broken action phase, unrelated background perspective, lighting reset, text flicker, cut, or other obvious boundary discontinuity. Near-still, moving-camera, and changing-subject-scale results are all valid when the join is natural.

## Bulsong Prompt Shape

```text
Use the uploaded first-frame image as the exact starting frame.
Use this motion prompt only if the human explicitly changes the standing 불송 still-image rule for the current request.
It is a mainstream Korean-song artwork from the approved current-release cover: preserve its photorealistic, anime, cinematic, surreal, fashion-editorial, or object-led visual lane without adding Buddhist or devotional cues.
Create one uninterrupted calm environmental take from a locked-off camera.
Keep the same composition, crop, camera distance, lighting, palette, subject placement, and typography from first frame to final frame.
Animate only gentle ambient details already present in the image: city rain, window reflections, fabric movement, hair movement, passing light, dust in room light, page-edge movement, or slow screen glow.
Keep adult human subjects, faces, hands, clothing, objects, and the mainstream music mood stable.
If the human explicitly requested Korean image text for this release, keep that existing short phrase stable and readable. Otherwise keep the frame text-free. Do not invent any channel name.
No new text, no subtitles, no lyrics, no UI, no added logos, no scene changes, no religious or devotional symbols, no nudity, no minors, and no celebrity lookalikes.
```

## Other Channels Or Explicit Requests

- If the selected channel has no profile, derive a visual system from the channel purpose, release concept, and human request.
- Do not borrow another channel's fixed signature unless the human explicitly asks.
- Keep metadata titles broad, natural, and clickable. Visual scene details can support the description, but the public title should normally lead with genre/lane and listening use case.
