# OpenClaw Visual Asset Skills

Use this when OpenClaw creates cover images, YouTube thumbnails, short loop videos, or still-image render packages for this repo.

The production source of truth is [openclaw-channel-profiles](openclaw-channel-profiles/README.md). Run `scripts/openclaw-release channel-profile` first, then read the returned `profile_doc`.

## Shared Asset Contract

- Create static cover and thumbnail images with OpenAI GPT Image models, not Dreamina.
- Use Gemini first for moving loop videos, then Dreamina/Seedance when Gemini is unavailable, blocked after retries, or in its 24 hour cooldown after 3 successful video generations. If Dreamina/Seedance is blocked by credit, account, quota, CAPTCHA, browser, or provider failure and Gemini has already used all 3 successful videos in the active 24 hour window, use the approved `still_image` fallback instead of deferring. HaruHaru, sundaze, Solwave Radio, Tokyo Daydream Radio photorealistic Japanese hip-hop/R&B/rap releases, Club Bloom, and 불송 skip provider loop videos during normal automation and use `still_image` render.
- Always create the final 16:9 cover/first-frame first, then create the YouTube thumbnail from that image as a reference/edit derivative.
- Keep cover, thumbnail, and loop video or still-image render visuals visually continuous: same subject count, subject placement, silhouettes, clothing colors, props, background landmarks, lighting, palette, and camera angle.
- Do not put the YouTube channel name, channel logo, or channel-brand line on covers, thumbnails, first-frame images, or loop videos.
- If text is useful, use only a short natural style, genre, use-case, or passage phrase. Examples: `J-POP`, `LOFI`, `TECH HOUSE`, `CINEMATIC ORCHESTRA`, `GAME OST`, `Genesis 1:1-5`, `Matthew 1:18-25`, `팔정도 명상팝`, `자비 트립합`. The phrase should feel integrated into the image, not like a pasted badge. All cover/thumbnail/first-frame text must have a transparent background: letters sit directly on the artwork, with readability coming from font weight, color, subtle shadow, thin outline, or local contrast only. Do not create black boxes, semi-transparent dark panels, white or colored rectangles, gradient scrims, stickers, badges, pills, capsules, or any filled label shape behind text; reject/regenerate if a thumbnail has one.
- Provider loop videos must preserve that transparent-text treatment. If the first frame already contains a short phrase, keep the letters directly on the scene; do not add, thicken, animate in, or preserve any black box, translucent dark panel, gradient scrim, rectangle, sticker, badge, pill, capsule, or filled label shape behind the text. Reject/regenerate provider clips that wrap existing text in a new panel even when the still image was clean.
- Do not add title sentences, lyrics, subtitles, duration text, UI, logos, watermarks, or unrelated words to the cover/first-frame or loop video.
- If Gemini/Veo adds its own provider logo or watermark, usually in the bottom-right corner, accept it as an unavoidable provider artifact. Do not regenerate only because that provider logo is present.
- Do not include spectrum bars, waveform graphics, equalizers, or audio meters in generated assets. The app adds the audio-reactive visualizer during final render.
- Available app-rendered visualizer presets are `bars`, `mirror-bars`, `calm-bars`, and `none`. `bars` and `mirror-bars` render center-weighted Gaussian EQ bar groups whose columns bounce in place; they should not look like a flowing waveform or dense left-to-right equalizer sweep. `calm-bars` is the same idea with fewer, lower-opacity, very low-motion bars. Do not use removed busy presets: small dots/particles, thin waveform, multiwave, radial, pulse, or the spectrum style used on `창세기 창조의 빛`. `BibliaCanto` must use `none`; `불송` must use `calm-bars`.
- Human visual requests override the channel default when they are safe and compatible with the channel.
- All generated visuals should look animated, anime, illustrated, or stylized unless a channel profile says otherwise. `Soft Hour Radio` uses high-resolution photorealistic real-world BGM environments by default, with locked-off camera video and no camera movement. `HaruHaru` uses photorealistic Korean lifestyle still images by default. `sundaze` uses photorealistic English/American friend-taken smartphone lifestyle still images by default. `Solwave Radio` uses photorealistic Latin/Spanish friend-taken smartphone lifestyle still images by default. `Club Bloom` uses photorealistic premium nightlife still images by default. `Cinematic Pulse` uses original photorealistic cinematic first-frame art and restrained cinematic motion. `불송` uses app-rendered still images with respectful contemporary female-led Buddhist music scenes by default: clearly adult temple-stay women, Hongdae/Seoul streetwear women practitioners/listeners, Buddha/statue plus a modern woman listener, two adult women for duet songs, or female-coded animated/stylized Buddhist practitioner concepts. Do not use men or male monks unless the human explicitly asks for a male subject in the current request. Avoid repeating a fixed old devotional scene.

## Loop Video Rules

- Before clicking Generate in Gemini, Dreamina, or Seedance, record the provider attempt with `scripts/openclaw-release provider-video-start --release-id RELEASE_ID --first-frame ABSOLUTE_FIRST_FRAME_IMAGE_PATH --provider gemini|dreamina|seedance`. This command hashes the first-frame image and blocks duplicate same-release/same-image provider starts while another provider is still inside its wait window.
- Gemini is the first provider for moving-video channels. After starting a Gemini generation, wait for Gemini to finish instead of immediately opening Dreamina/Seedance with the same image. Keep the OpenClaw lock alive with heartbeats while waiting.
- Check waiting state with `scripts/openclaw-release provider-video-status --release-id RELEASE_ID --first-frame ABSOLUTE_FIRST_FRAME_IMAGE_PATH`. If `wait_remaining_seconds` is greater than 0, keep waiting and do not start Dreamina/Seedance. Only after 20 minutes with no usable Gemini MP4 may OpenClaw mark Gemini timed out and start Dreamina/Seedance for the same image.
- When Gemini succeeds, run `scripts/openclaw-release provider-video-finish --release-id RELEASE_ID --first-frame ABSOLUTE_FIRST_FRAME_IMAGE_PATH --provider gemini --status succeeded --output-video ABSOLUTE_GEMINI_MP4` and upload that MP4. When Gemini rejects the prompt immediately, mark it `failed` and retry Gemini with a safer prompt when quota remains. When Gemini is still generating after 20 minutes, mark it `timed_out`, then start Dreamina/Seedance. Never run Gemini and Dreamina/Seedance concurrently for the same release and first-frame image.
- Normal Dreamina/Seedance browser clips use `1.0 Fast`, first-frame/start-frame only, no Omni Reference, no last-frame/end-frame reference, `16:9`, `720p`, exactly `10 seconds`.
- If the Seedance/Dreamina duration control is hidden when the screen opens, gently drag/scroll the settings/control row to the right until the duration option is visible, then set it to exactly `10 seconds`. Do not Generate while the duration is hidden, set to 5 seconds, switched to `2.0 Fast`, or uncertain.
- Do not upload both first and last frames for Dreamina/Seedance. Adding a last-frame reference makes Dreamina switch back to `2.0 Fast` and wastes Seedance credits.
- HaruHaru normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload photorealistic still cover/thumbnail images, then render with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 1080p --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style editorial-lower-left`.
- sundaze normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload photorealistic English/American friend-taken phone-photo still cover/thumbnail images, then render with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 1080p --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style editorial-lower-left`.
- Solwave Radio normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload photorealistic friend-taken phone-photo still cover/thumbnail images, then render with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 1080p --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style editorial-lower-left`. Solwave MP4 visuals use the still image plus lower-left lyrics and lower-right spectrum by default.
- Club Bloom normal releases do not use provider clips. Upload photorealistic club/bar/lounge/festival still cover/thumbnail images with attractive clearly adult women in revealing YouTube-safe nightlife outfits, then render with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 1080p --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars`. Club Bloom MP4 visuals use the still image plus lower-right spectrum by default.
- Gemini clips are uploaded as generated after inspection. Do not mention duration in Gemini prompts. Tag them with `--loop-video-provider gemini`; the app will render Gemini-tagged clips with a 2.0 second crossfade.
- Do not put `10 seconds`, `5 seconds`, `16:9`, `720p`, `1080p`, `loop`, `seamless loop`, `repeat`, or `cyclic` in the generation prompt. Set those in provider controls when available.
- Do not use a local pan/zoom, app still-image animation, or other workaround when video generation fails unless the human explicitly approves a still-image fallback.

## Prompt Stability Rules

- Describe the visible state, camera, composition, and environmental motion. Avoid conceptual phrases that imply a staged montage.
- Avoid `playlist`, `music visual`, `visualizer shot`, and scripture-framework words in the video prompt when they are not visually necessary. For example, do not prompt `Four Noble Truths playlist`; for 불송 prompt the visible scene instead, such as `a clearly adult temple-stay woman in modern streetwear with headphones and prayer beads, quietly facing a Buddha statue in a rain-washed temple courtyard, cinematic natural phone-photo realism, warm lantern glow, soft incense haze`.
- Prefer positive fixed-shot language: `single fixed camera shot`, `locked-off camera`, `one uninterrupted calm environmental take`, `same composition from first to last frame`.
- Keep negative lists short. Too many `do not` clauses can make providers focus on the forbidden action and cause hard cuts, resets, or layout changes.
- Ask for ambient motion that naturally exists in the first frame: light flicker, rain ripple, incense smoke, water shimmer, fabric/hair wind, city lights, DJ LEDs, lantern glow, leaves, clouds, or reflections.
- Reject/regenerate clips that change to a different scene, reset composition mid-clip, introduce extra text, invent channel names, or make the subject noticeably zoom in/out when the profile requires stable scale.
- Reject/regenerate clips that add a black or semi-transparent background behind first-frame text, turn plain integrated typography into a badge/label, or make the text flicker between transparent and boxed states.

## Bulsong Prompt Shape

```text
Use the uploaded first-frame image as the exact starting frame.
Use this motion prompt only if the human explicitly changes the standing 불송 still-image rule for the current request.
It is a respectful contemporary Buddhist music artwork: a clearly adult woman practitioner/listener, two adult women for a duet song, female-coded stylized Buddhist practitioner/nun, or Buddha/statue-plus-modern-woman scene with a subtle music cue in a temple, meditation room, temple courtyard, or Buddhist street/temple hybrid setting.
Create one uninterrupted calm environmental take from a locked-off camera.
Keep the same composition, crop, camera distance, lighting, palette, subject placement, and typography from first frame to final frame.
Animate only gentle ambient details already present in the image: lantern flicker, candle glow, incense smoke, dust in window light, garden rain, page-edge movement, robe fabric settling, or slow reflections.
Keep the adult human subject, Buddha/statue if present, serene faces, hands, Buddhist setting, and subtle music-listening cue stable and respectful.
Keep the existing short Korean phrase stable and readable if present. Do not invent any channel name.
No men, no male monks, no male rappers, no male listeners, no new text, no subtitles, no lyrics, no UI, no added logos, no scene changes, no singing, no dancing, no comedic gadget focus, no exaggerated divine effects, and no disrespectful religious imagery.
```

## Other Channels Or Explicit Requests

- If the selected channel has no profile, derive a visual system from the channel purpose, release concept, and human request.
- Do not borrow another channel's fixed signature unless the human explicitly asks.
- Keep metadata titles broad, natural, and clickable. Visual scene details can support the description, but the public title should normally lead with genre/lane and listening use case.
