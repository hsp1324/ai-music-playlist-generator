# OpenClaw Channel Genre Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy when planning every playlist release. Each release chooses one detailed video style lane, keeps all tracks close to that lane, and is assigned to one broader YouTube playlist bucket.

Do not make one release a sampler of unrelated styles. Slight variation inside the lane is fine: for example, a HaruHaru trap K-pop release may vary drum density, vocal tone, or chorus energy, but it should not mix trap, ballad, pop-rock, and R&B in the same rendered video.

## Operating Rule

- Pick a channel first, then pick one broad genre bucket and one detailed style lane before Suno generation.
- The YouTube video title/description/thumbnail phrase should name the detailed lane when it is useful: `K-POP Trap`, `Boom Bap K-POP`, `Bachata Pop`, `Tech House`, `Lofi Study BGM`, etc.
- The app assigns the uploaded video to a broader YouTube playlist bucket: trap and boom bap both go into `K-pop Hip-Hop`; tech house and deep house both go into `House Music`.
- Reused tracks must match the selected lane. If the reuse pool changes the actual sound, retitle the release to the true majority lane or choose different reused tracks.
- Rotate across underused buckets for each channel before repeating a recently used lane.

## HaruHaru

Broad YouTube buckets and detailed video lanes:

- `K-pop Hip-Hop`: K-pop trap, K-pop boom bap, rap-pop, 808 confidence pop, dark street-pop with rap verses.
- `Korean R&B`: Korean R&B pop, K-pop soul, neo-soul pop.
- `K-pop Dance-Pop`: idol-pop, performance dance-pop, running/workout K-pop. Secondary for HaruHaru; prefer hip-hop/R&B lanes unless explicitly requested.
- `K-pop Synth-Pop`: neon drive synth-pop and explicit HaruHaru city-pop/city-pop influenced K-pop when the human asks or an existing release is already city-pop. For non-city-pop HaruHaru lanes, do not use city-pop tracks as backfill.
- `K-pop Pop-Rock`: guitar pop, pop-rock drive, weekend band-pop. Secondary for HaruHaru.
- `K-pop Ballads`: ballad-pop, breakup pop, piano/guitar emotional pop. Secondary for HaruHaru.
- `K-pop Mixes`: only when the sound is broad mainstream K-pop and no tighter lane is truthful. Avoid this as a planning target.

## Soft Hour Radio

Broad YouTube buckets and detailed video lanes:

- `Piano BGM`: default and preferred bucket for Soft Hour. Detailed lanes include cafe solo piano, rainy reading piano, quiet work piano, morning focus felt piano, fireplace piano, and warm room piano.
- `Sleep & Relax BGM`: only when the music is still solo piano but the use case is sleep/rest/relaxation, such as deep night felt piano or soft bedtime piano.
- `Study & Focus BGM`: only when the music is still solo piano but the primary hook is study/work/focus/reading.

Do not use `Lofi BGM`, `Jazz BGM`, `Acoustic BGM`, or `Calm BGM` as new Soft Hour planning targets unless the human explicitly changes the channel direction later. New Soft Hour tracks should not be lofi beats, Rhodes/jazz, guitar/acoustic ensemble, ambient pads, or generic mixed-instrument calm BGM.

## sundaze

Broad YouTube buckets and detailed video lanes:

- `Pop Hip-Hop`: rap-pop, trap-pop, sung-rap, 808 confidence pop.
- `Pop R&B`: Pop R&B, soul-pop, neo-soul pop.
- `Dance-Pop`: party warmup, rooftop dance-pop, workout/running pop.
- `Synth-Pop`: neon drive, city lights, polished synth-pop.
- `Pop-Rock`: guitar pop, road-trip pop-rock, weekend pop-rock.
- `Country Pop`: country pop road trip, small-town summer pop.
- `Americana & Folk Pop`: Americana pop, folk-pop, singer-songwriter pop.
- `Indie & Alt Pop`: indie pop, bedroom pop, alt-pop.
- `Acoustic & Ballad Pop`: rainy bedroom, heartbreak acoustic pop, ballad-pop.
- `Afropop & Amapiano`: English-forward Afropop, Afrobeats, Amapiano-pop.
- `English Pop`: fallback for broad feel-good English/American pop when no tighter bucket is truthful.

## Solwave Radio

Broad YouTube buckets and detailed video lanes:

- `Reggaeton & Urbano`: reggaeton pop, reggaeton suave, urbano latino.
- `Bachata Pop`: bachata pop romance, tropical rain bachata.
- `Salsa & Cumbia Pop`: salsa pop, cumbia pop, plaza/festival Latin groove.
- `Latin R&B & Soul`: Latin R&B, Spanish R&B, Latin soul, neo-soul pop.
- `Latin Pop-Rock`: pop-rock latino, guitar-driven Latin pop.
- `Pop Latino`: warm Latin pop, Spanish pop, verano latino when no tighter bucket is more accurate.

## Club Bloom

Broad YouTube buckets and detailed video lanes:

- `House Music`: deep house, tech house, progressive house, future house.
- `Bass & Garage`: bass house, UK garage, darker shuffling bass.
- `Drum & Bass`: drum and bass, liquid DnB.
- `Techno`: melodic techno, peak-time techno.
- `Trance`: trance, progressive trance.
- `Afro & Tropical House`: Afro house, tropical house, beach club house.
- `Synthwave Club`: synthwave club, retro neon night-drive club.
- `EDM & Festival`: trap EDM, electro house, big-room/festival EDM.

## Tokyo Daydream Radio

- `J-pop City & Synth-Pop`: city-pop, synth-pop, neon drive J-pop.
- `J-pop Dance-Pop`: rooftop/night-out/idol dance-pop.
- `J-pop Pop-Rock`: guitar pop, campus pop-rock, weekend band-pop.
- `Anime & Arcade Pop`: anime-pop, arcade/game-center J-pop.
- `J-pop Mixes`: broad mainstream J-pop only when no tighter lane is truthful.

## Cinematic Pulse

- `Dark Fantasy Cinematic`: dark fantasy orchestra, final battle fantasy.
- `Sci-Fi Action Cinematic`: sci-fi action, cyber chase, combat score.
- `Emotional Film Score`: emotional piano/strings, hopeful cinematic score.
- `Mystery & Tension Score`: mystery, suspense, dark investigation music.
- `Epic Trailer Music`: epic orchestra, trailer, heroic battle music.

## Storylight OST

- `Cute Game OST`: cute arcade, playful game OST.
- `Fantasy RPG OST`: fantasy RPG town, village, journey.
- `Anime Game BGM`: Japanese-style anime/game BGM.
- `Cozy Story OST`: cozy, whimsical, storybook music.

## BibliaCanto And 불송

BibliaCanto keeps the existing Old/New Testament passage playlist plus one modern style playlist such as `Scripture Hip-Hop Songs`, `Scripture R&B Songs`, `Bible K-Pop Songs`, `Scripture Trap Songs`, `Bible Neo-Soul Songs`, `Bible Afropop Songs`, or `Scripture Synth-Pop Songs`. New BibliaCanto releases must not use Gospel, worship, hymn, choir, praise-band, CCM, or church-service style buckets.

불송 buckets:

- `Buddhist Hip-Hop`: 불교 힙합, 불경 힙합, mindful hip-hop, Korean Buddhist rap, mellow boom bap, Buddhist hip-hop soul, restrained Buddhist trap-soul.
- Non-hip-hop 불송 buckets are allowed only when the human explicitly asks or when finishing an already-started release in that lane. Do not use obscure coined genre labels in public titles, thumbnail phrases, descriptions, or tags.
