# OpenClaw Channel Profile: 불송

Use this profile only after channel selection returns `불송`, or when the human explicitly asks for Korean vocal music internally inspired by Buddhist sources.

## Routing Contract

- This profile is for Korean vocal releases internally inspired by Buddhist sources/themes, but the public song should feel like broadly accessible modern Korean music, not a Buddhist-content package. The source/theme stays only in planning and app metadata as raw inspiration for better lyrics. New sung lyrics, public titles, descriptions, tags, covers, thumbnails, and still images should avoid Buddhist vocabulary and Buddhist-looking visual cues unless the human explicitly asks for a source-explicit legacy package. Music and artwork should vary aggressively from release to release. Hip-hop/rap remains an important high-performing lane, but OpenClaw may also choose R&B, quiet piano vocal ballads, trip-hop, glitch-hop, drum and bass, hyperpop, art-pop, psychedelic soul, avant-jazz rap, dark pop, new-age electronica, cinematic trap, spoken-word rap, or other coherent modern lanes when they fit the emotional theme and do not sound like old devotional music.
- Do not route Bible, Gospel, Old Testament, or New Testament worship here. Those now upload to `BibliaCanto`.
- Do not route normal Korean pop/hip-hop here unless the lyric plan is internally based on a Buddhist source/theme. The finished lyrics should translate that source into ordinary emotional language. Normal Korean pop and hip-hop with no internal Buddhist source/theme belong on `HaruHaru`.
- Publish through the app on the `불송` channel. The app schedules 불송 releases as public daily at 07:00 Asia/Seoul. Do not force private visibility unless the human explicitly pauses public scheduling.

## Music And Suno Style

- Choose either one clear modern release-level lane or one coherent mainstream 불송 vocal family before Suno generation. Rap, hip-hop, R&B, soul, and quiet piano vocal ballads may be mixed when the mood is connected; they do not need to be separated by narrow genre for 불송.
- 불송 is now diversity-first and audience-first. Do not keep making the same source-explicit Buddhist hip-hop release just because it worked once. Rotate high-contrast lanes across releases: boom bap, drill/trap-soul, dark R&B, quiet piano vocal ballad, trip-hop, glitch-hop, drum and bass, UK garage, phonk, hyperpop, psychedelic soul, avant-jazz rap, spoken-word rap, new-age electronica, cinematic trap, art-pop, or other clear contemporary styles. The constant is an emotionally useful Korean song inspired by the source/theme, not Buddhist words in the lyric and not one fixed beat palette.
- New default 불송 lyrics must avoid overt Buddhist vocabulary and scripture/source names. Do not put words such as `불교`, `불경`, `법구경`, `금강경`, `반야심경`, `법화경`, `경전`, `부처`, `부처님`, `보살`, `스님`, `법문`, `자비`, `가르침`, `업`, `업보`, `윤회`, `해탈`, `열반`, `사성제`, `팔정도`, `정견`, `정사유`, `정어`, `정업`, `정명`, `정정진`, `정념`, `정정`, `무상`, `무아`, `번뇌`, or `수행` in the sung Korean lyrics unless the human explicitly requests that vocabulary for the current release. Translate the idea into universal pop language: breath, night, worry, anger, letting go, being lighter, speaking gently, starting again, not holding on, and becoming okay.
- Public Suno style strings may mention the actual music lane and vocal tone, but avoid source-explicit labels such as `Buddhist vocal`, `Buddhist hip-hop`, `Dharma pop`, or `Dhammapada` for new mainstream releases. Keep source/theme notes in the workspace plan or app metadata instead.
- The visual diversity rule does not restrict the music vocals. 불송 audio may use male lead vocals, male duet, male/female duet, female lead vocals, or mixed vocals when the song concept benefits from it.
- For every 불송 vocal Suno style string, include a concrete Korean lead-vocal tone or delivery phrase, not just the genre. Rotate the voice concept per track so the songs do not all sound like the same Suno singer. Use a specific palette such as `breathy introspective Korean female alto, close-mic, light rasp`, `calm low-register Korean rap with dry spoken diction`, `warm soulful male baritone vocal, soft vibrato`, `two warm male rappers trading reflective verses, one low and one nasal mid-range`, `airy female hook vocal with restrained boom-bap spoken rap`, `soft nasal indie-R&B female vocal, intimate mic`, or `warm male/female duet with male baritone verses and airy female chorus`. Avoid repeating generic `Korean vocal`, `male rap`, `female vocal`, or `Buddhist vocal` wording across the whole release.
- Quiet piano ballads are valid 불송 songs: use simple piano accompaniment, gentle Korean lead vocal, an intimate emotional arc, and no beat drop or drum entry. Make it clear that this is a vocal song, not instrumental piano BGM, no-vocal relaxation music, or Soft Hour-style study piano.
- In the Suno custom lyrics field, add the matching short voice cue near the top, for example `[Lead vocal: breathy introspective Korean female alto, close-mic, light rasp]`. For duets, mark sections clearly with concise cues such as `[Male verse: warm low baritone rap, calm diction]` and `[Female hook: airy intimate R&B vocal]`. Keep the cues short and musical so Suno treats them as direction, not sung prose.
- Voice-tone cues are required guidance, but do not add a mandatory full-verse listening gate because it slows production. Use lightweight QC only: confirm the Suno setup is vocal/lyrics-based, avoid instrumental/no-vocal settings, and reject/regenerate only when the UI preview, filename/metadata, or a very quick spot check makes it obvious that the audio is instrumental, pad-only, hum-only, missing a lead voice, or otherwise not a usable vocal/rap result.
- Do not use obscure coined genre names in YouTube titles, thumbnail phrases, descriptions, or tags. For new mainstream releases, use plain song-first wording such as `힙합`, `R&B`, `랩`, `소울`, `트립합`, `아트팝`, or `밤에 듣는 노래` without source words. Source-explicit labels such as `불교 힙합`, `불경 힙합`, `Buddhist hip-hop`, or `Korean Buddhist rap` are legacy/explicit mode only.
- Avoid trot, ppongjjak, and old Korean cabaret-pop completely unless the human explicitly asks for them. Do not use Korean trot rhythm, two-beat ppongjjak bounce, trot vocal ornaments, accordion/brass trot clichés, nightclub/cabaret trot arrangement, or old-TV trot mood.
- In Suno Advanced Options / excluded styles, include blockers such as `trot, Korean trot, ppongjjak, 뽕짝, 트로트, trot vocal, cabaret trot, old Korean trot, two-beat trot bounce, accordion trot, brass trot`.
- If a generated track comes out with strong trot/ppongjjak feel, reject it and regenerate in a clearer modern lane.
- Build each playlist around a strong first block. Generate roughly 10 minutes of new high-confidence Korean vocal music first, with the best hook/rap/vocal entry as track 1 and no long slow intro before the music identity is clear.
- To reach roughly one hour, use existing approved 불송 tracks from the same mainstream vocal pool. Rap, hip-hop, R&B, soul, and quiet piano vocal ballads may fill the same release when the emotional mood fits, with liked tracks first. Keep reused/fallback tracks after the fresh lead block and keep the strongest/most title-relevant lane near the front. Exclude disliked/copyright-blocked/reuse-disabled tracks. Do not add unrelated jazz, trot, guitar-folk/acoustic, cinematic, instrumental BGM, no-vocal piano, or generic meditation tracks just to hit one hour. A shorter coherent release is better than a one-hour mismatched release.
- When a single track has an unusually strong hook, replay signal, or human praise, make it a standalone single candidate and also consider using it as a future same-lane playlist lead track.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: modern, clickable, emotionally direct, and deliberately varied. The fixed identity is the channel's internal lyric-inspiration process, not a visible Buddhist brand. Public visuals should look like mainstream Korean song artwork, R&B/hip-hop/ballad/trip-hop album art, or a strong emotional lifestyle image. Do not make the image look Buddhist by default.
- Current thumbnail research: YouTube's own guidance says thumbnails and titles are the first decision surface, custom thumbnails are common among top-performing videos, simple dynamic color/composition beats over-complex design, thumbnails must work across devices, and styles should keep adapting to what works in the audience's community. For 불송, old religious cues such as statue-only, sutra-desk, temple, lotus, monk, and prayer imagery make the channel look niche and repetitive. The more clickable direction is a strong mainstream subject, unusual contrast, modern emotion, and a visual package that could stand beside current Korean R&B/hip-hop/ballad releases.
- Therefore 불송 visuals should optimize for a click-stopping mainstream song image first. Keep source names, chapter names, Buddhist terms, scripture/source phrases, and Buddhist objects out of new public title/description/tags and artwork unless the human explicitly asks for source-explicit wording or image text.
- Each new 불송 plan must include a `novelty_brief`: what visual lane is being used, what music lane is being used, and why it is not a repeat of the recent 불송 uploads. Check recent releases before generation. Avoid repeating the last 5 visual lanes, same face/body type, same prayer pose, same camera angle, same outfit, same temple set, same warm-gold palette, or same cover composition when possible.
- Do not use a previous 불송 release cover, thumbnail, final still, or bad rejected image as an image reference for a new release. Start each new release from a fresh text prompt plus a recent-release variation check. The only normal reference use is deriving the current release thumbnail from the current release's approved cover/first-frame so one package stays consistent.
- Rotate radically different non-Buddhist visual lanes. Valid lanes include photorealistic Korean street/lifestyle scenes, Hongdae/Seoul night portraits, rainy-window R&B emotion, late-night room or car scenes, dark fashion editorial, duet/couple tension, empty chair or phone-object album art, cinematic city stills, surreal/metaphysical nonreligious symbols, cubist-inspired emotional collage, cyber/neon heartbreak imagery, anime/illustrated modern Korean music art, premium cinematic still life, object-only symbolic covers, and bold album-cover graphic realism.
- Human subjects are allowed but not mandatory. If a photorealistic person appears, make them clearly adult and YouTube-safe. Stylish adult women can work well for mainstream click-through, but do not force every release to use a woman. Handsome adult men, duet/couple scenes, abstract, symbolic, surreal, animated, or object-led covers are also valid when they create a stronger click.
- Do not use monks, monastic figures, Buddha statues/faces, temple architecture, prayer poses, prayer beads, lotus imagery, mandala geometry, sutra pages/textures, incense, lantern shrines, Dharma wheels, Buddhist deity imagery, or obvious Buddhist ritual objects in new default 불송 visuals.
- Music-listening cues can be mainstream and subtle: headphones, earbuds, a phone, a car stereo, a mic held by a human subject, turntable silhouettes, waveform-like architecture, club/stage lighting, bedroom speakers, or a late-night studio mood. Keep them as ordinary music/lifestyle cues, not religious-fusion cues.
- The image may be photorealistic, cinematic-real, anime, painterly, cubist-inspired, metaphysical, surrealist, new-age abstract, psychedelic, glitch, graphic-poster-like, or mixed-media. Do not use exact living-artist names in generation prompts. If the human says `Picasso` or similar, translate it into safe descriptive language such as `cubist-inspired`, `fragmented planes`, `abstract portrait geometry`, or `metaphysical collage`.
- Background-only scenes are acceptable only when the symbolism is genuinely bold, nonreligious, and thumbnail-readable, such as a cracked mirror over a neon city, an empty late-night bus stop, rain on a car window, a floating phone with unread messages, a surreal room filled with blue light, or a cubist street/city collage. Reject plain temple paths, lotus ponds, lanterns, mountains, incense, abstract Buddhist symbols, empty shrines, or statue wallpapers.
- Avoid goofy parody, cheap mystic clipart, stale religious wallpaper, generic old sutra-desk scenes, real celebrity/idol lookalikes, minors, nudity, explicit sexuality, exaggerated divine glow, religious performance imagery, and disrespectful religious imagery.

## Cover

- Create one final 16:9 cover first.
- The cover is the playback visual and YouTube thumbnail base. It is also the still image source for the final app render.
- The cover should use one fresh mainstream song/art subject from the visual identity above, not a Buddhist scene.
- If replacing a bad 불송 cover/thumbnail, do not feed that old/bad image back as a reference unless the human explicitly says to preserve its composition. Regenerate from a new text prompt and then use the approved new cover as the thumbnail/still source.
- For any internal Buddhist source, including Diamond Sutra / 금강경, do not show the source visually. Choose a visual lane that matches the actual song emotion and genre: dark R&B city night, hip-hop street confidence, quiet piano-ballad loneliness, surreal letting-go symbolism, cubist-inspired identity fragments, rainy room introspection, or cinematic adult lifestyle imagery.
- Do not accept generic religious/Buddhist backgrounds as final 불송 cover art. A cover can be human-led, abstract, symbolic, surreal, animated, or object-led, but it must have a fresh mainstream music hook and a thumbnail-readable reason to click.
- Do not add the `불송` channel label, the channel name, a channel logo, title sentences, scripture/source labels, sutra paragraphs, lyrics, subtitles, UI, duration text, watermark-like marks, or unrelated words to the cover/first-frame by default.
- Default to text-free cover/thumbnail packages. Phrases such as `삼도 힙합`, `법구경`, `법구경 힙합`, `불경 힙합`, `마음챙김 랩`, `자비 힙합`, `팔정도`, `정어`, or `반야심경 랩` should stay out of new public metadata and image text unless the human explicitly asks for source-explicit packaging on the current release.
- If the human explicitly asks for text in the image, keep it to one short phrase, use transparent-background typography only, and put it in safe negative space without covering the main subject. Use font weight, color, subtle shadow, thin outline, or local contrast for readability; do not use hard black rectangles, semi-transparent dark panels, white or colored rectangles, gradient scrims, detached labels, stickers, badges, pills, capsules, or UI-like tags.

## YouTube Thumbnail

- Use the same final cover/first-frame composition as the YouTube thumbnail.
- Preserve the same subject, music cue, lighting, palette, and setting from the cover. The thumbnail can crop slightly closer for click clarity, but it must not become a separate unrelated portrait or a new scene.
- Do not create a separate channel-branded thumbnail. Do not add `불송`, channel labels, headline blocks, black text boxes, semi-transparent dark panels, white or colored rectangles, gradient scrims, hard rectangles, stickers, badges, pills, capsules, or detached label shapes. Any text that remains from the cover/first-frame must sit directly on the image with a transparent background.
- The thumbnail should usually be text-free and can be the exact same image as the cover. It should win through subject, color, emotion, and composition rather than through source labels or genre words.
- When using `scripts/openclaw-release auto-publish-playlist` or `auto-publish-single`, pass the same image as both `--cover` and `--thumbnail`, or use `--allow-cover-as-thumbnail` when the same image is intended.

## Still Image Render

- 불송 is always an app-rendered still-image video channel in normal automation. This is a hard channel rule, not a fallback for failed or quota-blocked video generation.
- Do not create, upload, or wait for a Gemini, Dreamina, or Seedance loop video for 불송 unless the human explicitly changes this standing 불송 still-image rule in the current request.
- Queue final renders with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 720p --video-spectrum-overlay-style calm-bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style center-breath-serif --lyrics-alignment-mode whisper`.
- Use the same mainstream non-Buddhist cover/first-frame composition as the rendered visual source and YouTube thumbnail base. The thumbnail may crop slightly closer, but it should not become a different scene.
- The app adds the low-motion `calm-bars` spectrum and centered `center-breath-serif` lyrics. Do not bake spectrum bars, waveform graphics, lyrics, subtitles, or karaoke text into the uploaded image.
- Do not add title sentences, large scripture text, duration text, UI, logos, religious imagery, exaggerated divine glow, idol/model posing, or comedic modern-gadget focus.
- Do not use conceptual visual words such as `playlist`, `music visual`, `visualizer shot`, `Four Noble Truths`, `사성제`, `Eightfold Path`, `팔정도`, `정어`, `Dharma`, `Buddhist`, `temple`, `Buddha`, or `lotus` in image prompts. Put the Buddhist source/theme only in internal metadata, not in the visible scene.
