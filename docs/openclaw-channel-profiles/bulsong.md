## Routing Contract

- This profile is for Korean vocal releases internally inspired by Buddhist sources/themes, but the public song should feel like broadly accessible modern Korean music, not a Buddhist-content package. The source/theme stays only in planning and app metadata as raw inspiration for better lyrics. New sung lyrics, public titles, descriptions, tags, covers, thumbnails, and still images should avoid Buddhist vocabulary and Buddhist-looking visual cues unless the human explicitly asks for a source-explicit legacy package. Music and artwork should vary aggressively from release to release. Hip-hop/rap remains an important high-performing lane, but OpenClaw may also choose R&B, piano-only vocal pop, acoustic-guitar-only vocal pop, fresh Korean indie-acoustic pop, trip-hop, glitch-hop, drum and bass, hyperpop, art-pop, psychedelic soul, avant-jazz rap, dark pop, new-age electronica, cinematic trap, spoken-word rap, or other coherent modern lanes when they fit the emotional theme and do not sound like old devotional music.
- Do not route Bible, Gospel, Old Testament, or New Testament worship here. Those now upload to `BibliaCanto`.
- Do not route normal Korean pop/hip-hop here unless the lyric plan is internally based on a Buddhist source/theme. The finished lyrics should translate that source into ordinary emotional language. Normal Korean pop and hip-hop with no internal Buddhist source/theme belong on `HaruHaru`.
- Publish through the app on the `불송` channel. The app schedules 불송 releases as public every Wednesday at 07:00 Asia/Seoul. Do not force private visibility unless the human explicitly pauses public scheduling.

- Choose either one clear modern release-level lane or one coherent mainstream 불송 vocal family before Suno generation. Rap, hip-hop, R&B, soul, and quiet piano vocal ballads may be mixed when the mood is connected; they do not need to be separated by narrow genre for 불송.

The constant is an emotionally useful Korean song inspired by the source/theme, not Buddhist words in the lyric and not one fixed sound palette.
- New default 불송 lyrics must avoid overt Buddhist vocabulary and scripture/source names. Do not put words such as `불교`, `불경`, `법구경`, `금강경`, `반야심경`, `법화경`, `경전`, `부처`, `부처님`, `보살`, `스님`, `법문`, `자비`, `가르침`, `업`, `업보`, `윤회`, `해탈`, `열반`, `사성제`, `팔정도`, `정견`, `정사유`, `정어`, `정업`, `정명`, `정정진`, `정념`, `정정`, `무상`, `무아`, `번뇌`, or `수행` in the sung Korean lyrics unless the human explicitly requests that vocabulary for the current release. - For every 불송 vocal Suno style string, include a concrete Korean lead-vocal tone or delivery phrase, not just the genre. Rotate the voice concept per track so the songs do not all sound like the same Suno singer. Use a specific palette such as `breathy introspective Korean female alto, close-mic, light rasp`, `calm low-register Korean rap with dry spoken diction`, `warm soulful male baritone vocal, soft vibrato`, `two warm male rappers trading reflective verses, one low and one nasal mid-range`, `airy female hook vocal with restrained boom-bap spoken rap`, `soft nasal indie-R&B female vocal, intimate mic`, or `warm male/female duet with male baritone verses and airy female chorus`. Fresh indie-acoustic may add only very light supporting rhythm. All three lanes still require a verified source-to-lyric map; the lighter sound must not flatten the source into generic comfort lyrics.

- Voice-tone cues are required guidance, but do not add a mandatory full-verse listening gate because it slows production. Use lightweight QC only: confirm the Suno setup is vocal/lyrics-based, avoid instrumental/no-vocal settings, and reject/regenerate only when the UI preview, filename/metadata, or a very quick spot check makes it obvious that the audio is instrumental, pad-only, hum-only, missing a lead voice, or otherwise not a usable vocal/rap result.

- If a generated track comes out with strong trot/ppongjjak feel, reject it and regenerate in a clearer modern lane.
- Build each playlist around a strong first block, with the best hook/rap/vocal entry as track 1 and no long slow intro before the music identity is clear. Continue creating high-confidence Korean vocal tracks as needed to meet the one-hour requirement.
- Existing approved 불송 tracks from the same mainstream vocal pool may fill the release when the emotional mood fits, with liked tracks first. Keep reused tracks after the fresh lead block and keep the strongest/most title-relevant lane near the front. Exclude disliked/copyright-blocked/reuse-disabled tracks. If matching reuse is scarce, follow [openclaw-one-hour-new-audio-policy.md](../openclaw-one-hour-new-audio-policy.md) and generate more same-family, same-theme Suno vocals until the playlist reaches one hour. ## Visual Identity

- Mood: modern, clickable, emotionally direct, and deliberately varied. The fixed identity is the channel's internal lyric-inspiration process, not a visible Buddhist brand. Public visuals should look like mainstream Korean song artwork, R&B/hip-hop/ballad/trip-hop album art, or a strong emotional lifestyle image. Do not make the image look Buddhist by default.
- Current thumbnail research: YouTube's own guidance says thumbnails and titles are the first decision surface, custom thumbnails are common among top-performing videos, simple dynamic color/composition beats over-complex design, thumbnails must work across devices, and styles should keep adapting to what works in the audience's community. For 불송, old religious cues such as statue-only, sutra-desk, temple, lotus, monk, and prayer imagery make the channel look niche and repetitive. The more clickable direction is a strong mainstream subject, unusual contrast, modern emotion, and a visual package that could stand beside current Korean R&B/hip-hop/ballad releases.
- Therefore 불송 visuals should optimize for a click-stopping mainstream song image first. Keep source names, chapter names, Buddhist terms, scripture/source phrases, and Buddhist objects out of new public title/description/tags and artwork unless the human explicitly asks for source-explicit wording or image text.
- Each new 불송 plan must include a `novelty_brief`: what visual lane is being used, what music lane is being used, and why it is not a repeat of the recent 불송 uploads. Check at least the latest eight covers when available. Avoid repeating the same hero subject, human type, pose, camera distance, setting, dominant palette, visual metaphor, or cover composition. Repetition is a warning to redesign the concept, not a reason to block the release or retry forever.
- Do not use a previous 불송 release cover, thumbnail, final still, or bad rejected image as an image reference for a new release. Start each new release from a fresh text prompt plus a recent-release variation check. Do not derive a separate thumbnail; use the current release's approved cover/first-frame file unchanged for both playback and YouTube.
- Before generation, read the actual source-to-lyric map, strongest track hooks, shared emotional arc, genre/arrangement, public title promise, and season or listening context. Write a short `visual_lyric_evidence` note with at least two concrete lyric details such as an action, place, object, weather condition, relationship moment, or change in emotion. The chosen scene must visibly use at least one of those details instead of replacing the lyrics with an unrelated symbol. Translate them into a visible idea with one focal subject, one action or tension, and one curiosity trigger that remains clear at thumbnail size.
- Draft at least three genuinely different candidates from different visual families before choosing the final direction. Use combinations such as a narrative moment, an object/metaphor, a designed place, a surreal transformation, an illustrated scene, a graphic collage, or a human-led editorial image. Three variants of the same portrait do not count.
- The candidate set must include at least one adult-woman-led cinematic scene and one illustrated or animated narrative scene with an adult woman when a human lead fits the lyrics. Prefer the candidate with a readable face, gesture, action, relationship tension, or sentimental moment when it gives the song a stronger small-thumbnail click reason. For a single adult lead, default to a clearly adult woman unless the human explicitly requests otherwise. Rotate age within an adult range, hairstyle, role, action, grouping, camera distance, medium, and emotional tone instead of producing interchangeable beauty portraits.
- Rotate radically different non-Buddhist visual lanes. Valid lanes include photorealistic Korean street/lifestyle scenes, R&B city nights, late-night rooms or cars, dark fashion editorial, relationship tension, cinematic city stills, surreal nonreligious symbols, cubist-inspired emotional collage, cyber/glitch imagery, anime/illustrated modern Korean music art, premium cinematic still life, object-only symbolic covers, designed environments, and bold album-cover graphic realism.

A woman is a story subject, not a generic beauty-portrait fallback: her visible action, setting, and emotion must come from the song. Choose nonhuman, spatial, illustrated, graphic, or symbolic concepts only when they create a more truthful and immediately readable click.
- Pure object, empty-room, geometric, or abstract concepts are secondary options. Do not select them merely because the metaphor is clever; they pass only when the emotion, event, and reason to click are as immediate at mobile-thumbnail size as the strongest human or animated narrative candidate.
- Choose the final candidate by source/lyric truthfulness, audio and genre fit, emotional specificity, small-thumbnail clarity, contemporary quality, recent novelty, and an honest click reason.
