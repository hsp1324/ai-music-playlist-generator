# OpenClaw Channel Profile: 불송

Use this profile only after channel selection returns `불송`, or when the human explicitly asks for Buddhist scripture-inspired music.

## Routing Contract

- This profile is for Korean vocal releases internally inspired by Buddhist sources/themes, but the public song should now feel like broadly accessible modern Korean music. The source/theme stays in planning and app metadata; new sung lyrics, public titles, descriptions, and tags should avoid overt Buddhist vocabulary unless the human explicitly asks for an old source-explicit package. Music and artwork should vary aggressively from release to release. Hip-hop/rap remains an important high-performing lane, but OpenClaw may also choose R&B, quiet piano vocal ballads, trip-hop, glitch-hop, drum and bass, hyperpop, art-pop, psychedelic soul, avant-jazz rap, dark pop, new-age electronica, cinematic trap, spoken-word rap, or other coherent modern lanes when they fit the emotional theme and do not sound like old devotional music.
- Do not route Bible, Gospel, Old Testament, or New Testament worship here. Those now upload to `BibliaCanto`.
- Do not route normal Korean pop/hip-hop here unless the lyrics are explicitly Buddhist scripture/teaching based. Normal Korean pop and hip-hop belong on `HaruHaru`.
- Publish through the app on the `불송` channel. The app schedules 불송 releases as public daily at 07:00 Asia/Seoul. Do not force private visibility unless the human explicitly pauses public scheduling.

## Music And Suno Style

- Choose either one clear modern release-level lane or one coherent mainstream 불송 vocal family before Suno generation. Rap, hip-hop, R&B, soul, and quiet piano vocal ballads may be mixed when the mood is connected; they do not need to be separated by narrow genre for 불송.
- 불송 is now diversity-first and audience-first. Do not keep making the same source-explicit Buddhist hip-hop release just because it worked once. Follow the global orchestral-vocal, piano-only-vocal, or acoustic-guitar-only-vocal policy and rotate emotional themes, singer identities, and compatible public flavors instead of selecting garage. The constant is an emotionally useful Korean song inspired by the source/theme, not Buddhist words in the lyric and not one fixed sound palette.
- New default 불송 lyrics must avoid overt Buddhist vocabulary and scripture/source names. Do not put words such as `불교`, `불경`, `법구경`, `금강경`, `반야심경`, `법화경`, `경전`, `부처`, `부처님`, `보살`, `스님`, `법문`, `자비`, `가르침`, `업`, `업보`, `윤회`, `해탈`, `열반`, `사성제`, `팔정도`, `무상`, `무아`, `번뇌`, or `수행` in the sung Korean lyrics unless the human explicitly requests that vocabulary for the current release. Translate the idea into universal pop language: breath, night, worry, anger, letting go, being lighter, speaking gently, starting again, not holding on, and becoming okay.
- Public Suno style strings may mention the actual music lane and vocal tone, but avoid source-explicit labels such as `Buddhist vocal`, `Buddhist hip-hop`, `Dharma pop`, or `Dhammapada` for new mainstream releases. Keep source/theme notes in the workspace plan or app metadata instead.
- The visual diversity rule does not restrict the music vocals. 불송 audio may use male lead vocals, male duet, male/female duet, female lead vocals, or mixed vocals when the song concept benefits from it.
- For every 불송 vocal Suno style string, include a concrete Korean lead-vocal tone or delivery phrase, not just the genre. Rotate the voice concept per track so the songs do not all sound like the same Suno singer. Use a specific palette such as `breathy meditative Korean female alto, close-mic, light rasp`, `calm low-register Korean rap with dry spoken diction`, `warm soulful male baritone vocal, soft vibrato`, `two warm male rappers trading reflective verses, one low and one nasal mid-range`, `airy female hook vocal with restrained boom-bap spoken rap`, `soft nasal indie-R&B female vocal, intimate mic`, or `warm male/female duet with male baritone verses and airy female chorus`. Avoid repeating generic `Korean vocal`, `male rap`, `female vocal`, or `Buddhist vocal` wording across the whole release.
- Quiet piano ballads are valid 불송 songs: use simple piano accompaniment, gentle Korean lead vocal, an intimate emotional arc, and no beat drop or drum entry. Make it clear that this is a vocal song, not instrumental piano BGM, no-vocal meditation music, or Soft Hour-style study piano.
- In the Suno custom lyrics field, add the matching short voice cue near the top, for example `[Lead vocal: breathy meditative Korean female alto, close-mic, light rasp]`. For duets, mark sections clearly with concise cues such as `[Male verse: warm low baritone rap, calm diction]` and `[Female hook: airy intimate R&B vocal]`. Keep the cues short and musical so Suno treats them as direction, not sung prose.
- Voice-tone cues are required guidance, but do not add a mandatory full-verse listening gate because it slows production. Use lightweight QC only: confirm the Suno setup is vocal/lyrics-based, avoid instrumental/no-vocal settings, and reject/regenerate only when the UI preview, filename/metadata, or a very quick spot check makes it obvious that the audio is instrumental, pad-only, hum-only, missing a lead voice, or otherwise not a usable vocal/rap result.
- Do not use obscure coined genre names in YouTube titles, thumbnail phrases, descriptions, or tags. For new mainstream releases, use plain song-first wording such as `힙합`, `R&B`, `랩`, `소울`, `트립합`, `아트팝`, or `밤에 듣는 노래` without source words. Source-explicit labels such as `불교 힙합`, `불경 힙합`, `Buddhist hip-hop`, or `Korean Buddhist rap` are legacy/explicit mode only.
- Avoid trot, ppongjjak, and old Korean cabaret-pop completely unless the human explicitly asks for them. Do not use Korean trot rhythm, two-beat ppongjjak bounce, trot vocal ornaments, accordion/brass trot clichés, nightclub/cabaret trot arrangement, or old-TV trot mood.
- In Suno Advanced Options / excluded styles, include blockers such as `trot, Korean trot, ppongjjak, 뽕짝, 트로트, trot vocal, cabaret trot, old Korean trot, two-beat trot bounce, accordion trot, brass trot`.
- If a generated track comes out with strong trot/ppongjjak feel, reject it and regenerate in a clearer modern lane.
- Build each playlist around a strong first block. Generate roughly 10 minutes of new high-confidence Korean vocal music first, with the best hook/rap/vocal entry as track 1 and no long slow intro before the music identity is clear.
- To reach roughly one hour, use existing approved 불송 tracks from the same mainstream vocal pool. Rap, hip-hop, R&B, soul, and quiet piano vocal ballads may fill the same release when the emotional mood fits, with liked tracks first. Keep reused/fallback tracks after the fresh lead block and keep the strongest/most title-relevant lane near the front. Exclude disliked/copyright-blocked/reuse-disabled tracks. Do not add unrelated jazz, trot, guitar-folk/acoustic, cinematic, instrumental BGM, no-vocal piano, or generic meditation tracks just to hit one hour. A shorter coherent release is better than a one-hour mismatched release.
- When a single track has an unusually strong hook, replay signal, or human praise, make it a standalone single candidate and also consider using it as a future same-lane playlist lead track.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: modern, clickable, respectful, and deliberately varied. The only fixed visual identity is a Buddhist source/theme. Do not lock 불송 into one repeated formula such as a pretty woman praying in front of a Buddha statue, a warm-gold temple, sutra pages, a monk desk, or a Buddha-only wallpaper.
- Current thumbnail research: YouTube's own guidance says thumbnails and titles are the first decision surface, custom thumbnails are common among top-performing videos, simple dynamic color/composition beats over-complex design, thumbnails must work across devices, and styles should keep adapting to what works in the audience's community. Recent Buddhist/lofi/hip-hop searches also show that statue-only, sutra-desk, and generic temple images blend together, while the more clickable direction is a strong subject, unusual contrast, modern emotion, and an instantly readable old-meets-new tension.
- Therefore 불송 visuals should optimize for a click-stopping image first, not for writing the scripture/source on the artwork. Keep source names, chapter names, and phrases such as `삼도 힙합`, `법구경`, `법구경 힙합`, or `반야심경 랩` out of new public title/description/tags and artwork unless the human explicitly asks for source-explicit wording or image text.
- Each new 불송 plan must include a `novelty_brief`: what visual lane is being used, what music lane is being used, and why it is not a repeat of the recent 불송 uploads. Check recent releases before generation. Avoid repeating the last 5 visual lanes, same face/body type, same Buddha close-up, same prayer pose, same camera angle, same outfit, same temple set, same warm-gold palette, or same cover composition when possible.
- Do not use a previous 불송 release cover, thumbnail, final still, or bad rejected image as an image reference for a new release. Start each new release from a fresh text prompt plus a recent-release variation check. The only normal reference use is deriving the current release thumbnail from the current release's approved cover/first-frame so one package stays consistent.
- Rotate radically different visual lanes. Valid lanes include photorealistic street-Buddhist fashion, Hongdae/Seoul night prayer-meets-rap scenes, temple-stay candid phone photos, cyber-Buddhist neon, surreal city/temple collision, metaphysical dream spaces, cubist-inspired Buddhist collage, new-age abstract lotus geometry, psychedelic mandala street art, dark fashion editorial, theatrical opera-stage Buddhist symbolism, anime or illustrated Buddhist fantasy, premium cinematic still life, object-only symbolic covers, and bold album-cover graphic realism.
- Human subjects are allowed but not mandatory. If a photorealistic person appears, make them clearly adult and YouTube-safe. Women can still work well, especially for stylish temple-stay, Hongdae, headphones, duet, or fashion/editorial concepts, but do not force every release to use a woman. Abstract, symbolic, surreal, animated, or object-led covers are also valid when they create a stronger click.
- If a monk or monastic figure appears, use respectful stylized/anime/illustrated or symbolic treatment unless the human explicitly asks for photorealism. Do not present any monastic figure as parody, caricature, idol/model fan service, or a disrespectful performer.
- A Buddha statue, Buddha face, lotus, prayer beads, sutra texture, temple architecture, mandala geometry, incense, or lantern light may appear, but the Buddhist cue should support a fresh music package rather than become the same old static devotional image.
- Music-listening cues must stay subtle and respectful. Headphones, earbuds, a small speaker, a mic held by a human subject, turntable silhouettes, waveform-like architecture, or stage lighting are allowed. Do not make the Buddha figure look like a commercial gadget mascot.
- The image may be photorealistic, cinematic-real, anime, painterly, cubist-inspired, metaphysical, surrealist, new-age abstract, psychedelic, glitch, graphic-poster-like, or mixed-media. Do not use exact living-artist names in generation prompts. If the human says `Picasso` or similar, translate it into safe descriptive language such as `cubist-inspired`, `fragmented planes`, `abstract portrait geometry`, or `metaphysical collage`.
- Background-only scenes are acceptable only when the symbolism is genuinely bold and thumbnail-readable, such as a cracked golden lotus mask over a neon city, a huge serene Buddha reflection in wet asphalt, a surreal floating prayer-bead planet, or a cubist temple/street collage. Reject plain temple paths, lotus ponds, lanterns, mountains, incense, abstract Buddhist symbols, empty shrines, or statue wallpapers that feel generic.
- Avoid goofy parody, cheap mystic clipart, stale statue wallpaper, generic old sutra-desk scenes, real celebrity/idol lookalikes, minors, nudity, explicit sexuality, exaggerated divine glow, singing/dancing Buddha depictions, and disrespectful religious imagery.

## Cover

- Create one final 16:9 cover first.
- The cover is the playback visual and YouTube thumbnail base. It is also the still image source for the final app render.
- The cover should use one fresh Buddhist music/art subject from the visual identity above, not the same old devotional scene by default.
- If replacing a bad 불송 cover/thumbnail, do not feed that old/bad image back as a reference unless the human explicitly says to preserve its composition. Regenerate from a new text prompt and then use the approved new cover as the thumbnail/still source.
- For Diamond Sutra / 금강경 covers, choose a visual lane that matches the actual song: bold hip-hop street Buddhism, metaphysical non-attachment, cubist-inspired illusion-of-self imagery, surreal lotus/city symbolism, dark R&B temple-night fashion, or a respectful adult temple-stay/practitioner scene. Do not default to a male monk portrait or a generic statue wallpaper.
- Do not accept generic scenery-only Buddhist backgrounds as final 불송 cover art. A cover can be human-led, abstract, symbolic, surreal, animated, or object-led, but it must have a fresh Buddhist music hook and a thumbnail-readable reason to click.
- Do not add the `불송` channel label, the channel name, a channel logo, title sentences, scripture/source labels, sutra paragraphs, lyrics, subtitles, UI, duration text, watermark-like marks, or unrelated words to the cover/first-frame by default.
- Default to text-free cover/thumbnail packages. Phrases such as `삼도 힙합`, `법구경`, `법구경 힙합`, `불경 힙합`, `마음챙김 랩`, `자비 힙합`, or `반야심경 랩` should stay out of new public metadata and image text unless the human explicitly asks for source-explicit packaging on the current release.
- If the human explicitly asks for text in the image, keep it to one short phrase, use transparent-background typography only, and put it in safe negative space without covering the main subject. Use font weight, color, subtle shadow, thin outline, or local contrast for readability; do not use hard black rectangles, semi-transparent dark panels, white or colored rectangles, gradient scrims, detached labels, stickers, badges, pills, capsules, or UI-like tags.

## YouTube Thumbnail

- Use the same final cover/first-frame composition as the YouTube thumbnail.
- Preserve the same subject, Buddhist cue, music cue, lighting, palette, and setting from the cover. The thumbnail can crop slightly closer for click clarity, but it must not become a separate unrelated portrait or a new scene.
- Do not create a separate channel-branded thumbnail. Do not add `불송`, channel labels, headline blocks, black text boxes, semi-transparent dark panels, white or colored rectangles, gradient scrims, hard rectangles, stickers, badges, pills, capsules, or detached label shapes. Any text that remains from the cover/first-frame must sit directly on the image with a transparent background.
- The thumbnail should usually be text-free and can be the exact same image as the cover. It should win through subject, color, emotion, and composition rather than through source labels or genre words.
- When using `scripts/openclaw-release auto-publish-playlist` or `auto-publish-single`, pass the same image as both `--cover` and `--thumbnail`, or use `--allow-cover-as-thumbnail` when the same image is intended.

## Still Image Render

- 불송 is always an app-rendered still-image video channel in normal automation. This is a hard channel rule, not a fallback for failed or quota-blocked video generation.
- Do not create, upload, or wait for a Gemini, Dreamina, or Seedance loop video for 불송 unless the human explicitly changes this standing 불송 still-image rule in the current request.
- Queue final renders with `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image --video-render-resolution 720p --video-spectrum-overlay-style calm-bars --lyrics-overlay --lyrics-overlay-style center-breath-serif --lyrics-alignment-mode whisper`.
- Use the same contemporary Buddhist cover/first-frame composition as the rendered visual source and YouTube thumbnail base. The thumbnail may crop slightly closer, but it should not become a different scene.
- The app adds the low-motion `calm-bars` spectrum and centered `center-breath-serif` lyrics. Do not bake spectrum bars, waveform graphics, lyrics, subtitles, or karaoke text into the uploaded image.
- Do not add title sentences, large scripture text, duration text, UI, logos, disrespectful religious imagery, exaggerated divine glow, idol/model posing, or comedic modern-gadget focus.
- Do not use conceptual visual words such as `playlist`, `music visual`, `visualizer shot`, `Four Noble Truths`, `사성제`, `Eightfold Path`, or `팔정도` in image prompts when a visible Buddhist scene description is enough. Put the Buddhist source/theme in metadata, not as invented background text.

## Metadata

- Use [../openclaw-youtube-metadata.md](../openclaw-youtube-metadata.md).
- Default metadata language should be Korean unless the human asks otherwise.
- Keep the selected Buddhist source/theme in internal planning notes and app metadata so the origin is traceable, but do not put it in the new default public title/description/tags. Use natural mainstream Korean title promises that ordinary listeners can click without needing Buddhist vocabulary, such as feeling lighter, calming a restless night, speaking more gently, letting go, starting over, or getting through a hard day.
- Package new uploads as accessible Korean vocal songs first. Use direct genre wording such as `힙합`, `랩`, `R&B`, `소울`, `트립합`, `아트팝`, or `노래`, plus a real emotional/use-case promise. Avoid source-heavy public phrases such as `불교 힙합`, `불경 힙합`, `법구경 힙합`, `반야심경 랩`, `Buddhist hip-hop`, or `Korean Buddhist rap` unless the human explicitly asks for a legacy/source-explicit release.
- Public titles no longer need to include the exact Buddhist source/chapter/section. Do not put `법구경`, `반야심경`, `금강경`, `법화경`, `팔정도`, `자비`, `무상`, or similar source/theme terms in new mainstream titles by default. Store the verified source/theme internally instead.
- Pair the internal source/theme with a practical emotional promise in planning, then express only the broad promise in the public title or first description sentence. Strong public shapes include `말이 날카로워질 때`, `잡고 있던 걸 놓는 밤`, `분노가 가라앉을 때까지`, `불안한 밤을 지나갈 때`, or `흔들릴 때 듣는`, as long as the lyrics and first tracks actually support that promise.
- Korean/default titles should not waste title space on redundant language labels such as `한국어 랩` or `한국어 힙합`. The channel is assumed to be Korean vocal/rap unless the human says otherwise. Use a song-first hook plus the real lane instead, such as `흔들리는 밤에 듣는 힙합`, `말을 삼키고 싶을 때 듣는 R&B`, or `가벼워지고 싶은 날의 랩`.
- If using a scripture source, name it carefully, such as Dhammapada-inspired, Heart Sutra-inspired, Diamond Sutra-inspired, Lotus Sutra-inspired, or Buddhist wisdom-inspired. Do not claim exact chapter/verse coverage unless verified.
- The description should not explain the song as a scripture paraphrase by default. Keep that source note internal unless the human asks for source-explicit packaging.
- Keep each release in one coherent emotional style family and name it naturally. Use public wording that normal viewers understand, such as `힙합`, `랩`, `R&B`, `소울`, `잔잔한 노래`, `피아노 발라드`, `트립합`, `아트팝`, or another plain label that truthfully matches the chosen package. If rap/R&B/piano ballad material is intentionally mixed, title it as a broader emotional song playlist rather than pretending every track is one narrow genre. Avoid obscure coined genre labels in public metadata; avoid trot/ppongjjak wording and sound unless the human explicitly asks for it.
- Do not relabel an already-rendered 불송 video as a different lane just because the current plan favors another style. If the finished video artwork/text and audio already point to Dharma pop / 다르마팝 / acoustic Dharma songs / 다르마송 / hip-hop / R&B / trip-hop, keep metadata truthful to that finished asset.
- If choosing a cute/gentle animation visual direction, choose a compatible music lane and make that lane clear in the title/description. The visual style and music style must feel like one package.
- Provide localized metadata for all configured languages, but keep Korean as the default top-level title/description.
- Uploads should be app-scheduled public daily at 07:00 Asia/Seoul unless the human explicitly pauses 불송 public scheduling.
- YouTube playlist assignment: already uploaded/source-explicit 불송 videos with Buddhist vocabulary in the public title should be placed in the YouTube playlist `불송`. New mainstream 불송 uploads should be placed in the YouTube playlist `노래`.
