## Routing Contract

- Explicit channel request wins.
- Cinematic Pulse is for no-vocal orchestra in a broad sense and for a separate cute game/anime lane. The orchestra lane includes chamber, lyrical, romantic, impressionistic, pastoral, neoclassical, gentle, comedic, emotional, game/film-score, heroic, sci-fi, dark-fantasy, and epic music. Piano is optional and epic scale is never required.
- Cinematic Pulse also receives former Storylight-style no-vocal game/anime/theme-park BGM because `Storylight OST` is manual-only. Treat that as a separate former-Storylight lane with truthful cute game/anime/arcade/theme-park packaging.
- Every release must store `cinematic_pulse_lane=orchestra` or `cinematic_pulse_lane=cute_game`. The app schedules orchestra at 07:00 and cute game at 19:00 Asia/Seoul using separate slots. Never let one lane's queued/scheduled count satisfy the other.
- Never cross-package lanes. If the audio is playful, cute, whimsical, cozy, arcade, anime side-story, item-shop, fantasy town, theme-park, or amusement-park BGM, do not use dark fantasy, cinematic battle, final battle, epic orchestra, or ominous movie-poster titles/thumbnail phrases. Retitle and re-visualize it as cute game/anime/theme-park BGM instead.
- If the title, thumbnail, or cover says `DARK FANTASY`, `CINEMATIC ORCHESTRA`, `EPIC BATTLE`, `TRAILER MUSIC`, or a similar serious film-score hook, every lead-block track must actually be cinematic/orchestral/film-score/game-orchestra enough to support that promise. Do not hide cute/playful fallback tracks at the front of a dark fantasy release.
- Do not use this profile for cozy fantasy, cafe/study BGM, EDM/house, or vocal pop.

## Visual Identity

- Mood: orchestral, emotional, intimate, elegant, playful, romantic, pastoral, mysterious, dramatic, heroic, gentle, bittersweet, lyrical, or high-stakes depending on the release.
- Cinematic Pulse has two approved visual lanes:
  - **Film-real lane:** photorealistic cinematic film-still / high-end movie-poster realism.
  - **Premium game-animation lane:** original high-end sci-fi/fantasy action-RPG key art with a beautiful adult heroine, elegant armor or futuristic fashion, dramatic game-cinematic lighting, and polished anime-real/game-animation rendering. This can evoke a modern console action RPG mood, but must not copy Stellar Blade, its characters, outfits, logos, UI, or exact composition.
- Quiet orchestral concepts may instead use contemporary editorial concert-hall, rehearsal-room, architecture, landscape, travel, seasonal lifestyle, or object-led imagery. Do not turn every orchestral cover into a battle poster. Keep all visual lanes modern and avoid old-fashioned classical-music clip art, parchment, ornate gold frames, or composer-bust clichés.
- The cover, thumbnail base, and loop video should feel like a dramatic movie frame, premium trailer shot, or high-end game key visual with cinematic lighting, depth of field, atmosphere, and believable materials.
- Do not use documentary-looking real war footage, news footage, real political imagery, celebrity likenesses, protected film/game characters, or exact franchise references. Photorealistic means original cinematic realism, not copied real media.
- Composition should read quickly on mobile: one strong focal scene, bold lighting, clear silhouettes, and high contrast.
- Visuals can be intense, but avoid gore, real-world political symbols, real war footage, and protected IP.
- App-rendered spectrum must use `bars` for Cinematic Pulse. Do not use `radial`, `multiwave`, `pulse`, small dots/particles, or busy waveform presets for this channel. Keep the bar spectrum clean, restrained, and non-cluttered.

## Render Visual

- Create a very high quality 16:9 cover/first-frame image, preferably 2560x1440 (`2k`) or at minimum 1920x1080. Use the film-real lane for movie/film-score concepts and the premium game-animation lane for game-orchestra, anime action-RPG, sci-fi heroine, or graceful fantasy game-score concepts.
- The uploaded first-frame image for the provider loop should be this cover image with any approved listener-promise text already integrated (or text-free).
- `Cinematic Pulse` is a Gemini moving-video channel for normal playlist automation. For every new Cinematic Pulse playlist, create one short Gemini/Veo image-to-video clip from the approved cover/first-frame whenever Gemini is available; do not silently render a still image while Gemini is usable.
- Natural first-to-last continuity is more important than whether the camera moves. A locked-off shot is valid, but smooth pan, dolly, truck, orbit, crane, or matched follow-camera movement is also valid when it produces a clean repeat boundary.
- Camera movement and subject scale/framing changes are allowed. A subject may grow or shrink in frame, approach or leave the camera, move across the composition, turn, or change pose when that motion serves the shot.
- Subjects may walk, turn, breathe, gesture, move fabric/hair, or perform another continuous action. Preserve identity and intentional scene logic. The final camera/subject motion must flow naturally into the opening action instead of visibly snapping, teleporting, or resetting.
- A transient subject may enter, cross the frame, and leave completely. For example, a bird may appear, fly through the scene, and disappear before the clip ends; if the same base landscape remains at the beginning and end, repeated playback can still look natural.
- Use continuous cinematic motion that can survive repetition: environmental movement such as smoke, fog, rain, embers, water, clouds, reflected light, or particles; a cyclic subject action; a matched tracking shot; or a gentle camera path whose end hands off naturally to its beginning.
- The last frame does not need to be pixel-identical to the first frame, and subject position, size, pose, camera angle, and camera distance may all change during the clip. Keep the underlying scene at the end broadly compatible with the beginning: similar base composition, background, camera viewpoint, lighting, and color state, with no large unrelated scene change. The acceptance test is whether the last-to-first transition looks natural during repeated playback.
- Do not ask Gemini to create a `loop`, `loop video`, `seamless loop`, `repeat`, or `cyclic` animation. Ask for one uninterrupted cinematic shot whose closing base scene is not greatly different from its opening base scene. This reduces provider-made resets or artificial loop tricks; the app owns the actual repetition and crossfade.
- Do not use Dreamina, Seedance, or CapCut for Cinematic Pulse. Gemini clips are uploaded as generated after inspection. If Gemini is unavailable, on cooldown, blocked after safe retries, or cannot create a usable MP4 within the wait window, use the approved still-image fallback instead of switching providers.
- Before upload, preview the downloaded MP4 at least three times back-to-back and inspect both loop boundaries. Reject/regenerate only when the join itself shows an obvious discontinuity such as a camera/subject teleport, incompatible direction or speed, broken action phase, unrelated background perspective, lighting reset, typography flicker, or scene cut. Do not reject camera movement, subject movement, or subject size/position/pose changes that play continuously and join naturally.
- Record the provider as Gemini when uploading:

```bash
scripts/openclaw-release upload-loop-video \
  --release-id RELEASE_ID \
  --loop-video ABSOLUTE_GEMINI_MP4 \
  --loop-video-provider gemini
```

- Queue final render with:

```bash
scripts/openclaw-release render-video \
  --release-id RELEASE_ID \
  --video-render-source-mode loop_video \
  --video-render-resolution 720p \
  --video-spectrum-overlay-style bars
```

- The app/render worker will use the uploaded loop video and add only the clean bar spectrum overlay. Do not bake spectrum graphics into the image or provider video itself.
- Use `--allow-still-image-video --video-render-source-mode still_image` when Gemini cannot produce a usable moving clip, or when the human explicitly approves a static-cover fallback for that release.

Gemini motion prompt shape:

```text
Use the uploaded Cinematic Pulse cover as the exact first frame.
Create one uninterrupted cinematic shot with natural first-to-last continuity.
The camera may stay locked or move with any smooth cinematic pan, tilt, tracking, dolly, truck, orbit, crane, zoom, or follow move.

Transient subjects may enter and leave naturally. For example, a bird may fly into the scene, cross the frame, and disappear before the end.
Preserve intentional scene logic. Finish on a base composition, background, camera viewpoint, lighting, and color state that is broadly similar to the opening base scene and not greatly different from it.
```

Describe the visible shot as above instead of putting `loop`, `loop video`, `seamless loop`, `repeat`, or `cyclic` in the Gemini prompt. Those conceptual words can make Gemini manufacture its own reset or artificial loop behavior. Provider duration, ratio, and quality belong in Gemini controls, not in the prompt.
